About Mamidi Nitesh

B.Tech CSE (AI & ML) | Aspiring Software Engineer | AI/ML Developer

Engineering Identity & Vision

I am Mamidi Nitesh, an undergraduate pursuing a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at Sri Chaitanya Institute of Technology & Research (affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad — JNTUH), graduating in 2027.

My work centers on designing practical software architectures that connect modern backend engineering with applied artificial intelligence. Rather than treating AI models as isolated black boxes, I focus on how they integrate into complete systems—spanning RESTful API design with FastAPI, local Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workflows, native Android assistant interfaces, and automated compliance pipelines.

Alongside building software applications, I maintain an independent research workflow exploring programming language design and API security through my experimental language project, Frisian / Farisan.

Core Focus Areas

01. BACKEND & API ENGINEERING

Modular Service Design

02. APPLIED AI & RAG PIPELINES

Context-Aware Intelligence

Mobile-Native Agent Systems

04. SECURITY & LANGUAGE RESEARCH

Experimental Compiler & Runtime Design

Investigating how security policies and API constraints can be expressed directly at the language syntax and parser level through Frisian/Farisan.
